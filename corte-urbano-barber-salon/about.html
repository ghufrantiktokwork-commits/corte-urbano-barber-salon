About us

Good music, good cuts

Corte Urbano Barber & Salon has been cutting hair on Chatham Drive for years. Customers describe a relaxed shop with good music, friendly staff and a clean space.

Our barbers are known for low fades, sharp line ups and custom designs. We cut for kids, teens and adults, and we welcome everyone. The shop is LGBTQ+ friendly.
